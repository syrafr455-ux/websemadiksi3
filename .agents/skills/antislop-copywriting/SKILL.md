# antislop-copywriting

> Anti Slop: Rules for AI Coding Agents. Copy & Text skill

Part of the antislop system. Deep-dives copy, headlines, CTAs, tone, and the patterns that make AI-written prose easy to spot.

## Principles
- **Never invent facts or numbers** (R-17, R-36, R-38).
- **Do not over-sterilize**: Keep an authentic, friendly human voice.

## Patterns to Avoid
- **Empty AI Vocabulary**: Avoid *unlock, elevate, empower, delve, showcase, robust, game-changer, seamless, cutting-edge, revolutionary* (R-16).
- **Significance Inflation**: Avoid *"the future of X"*, *"marking a pivotal moment"*, *"a new era of"* (R-36, C-5).
- **Empty Claims & Social Proof**: Avoid unverified claims like *"Trusted by thousands"* or fake ratings (R-17, R-18, C-5).
- **Staging & Triads**: Avoid forced three-part adjective lists (*"aman, cepat, terpercaya"*) or rhetorical contrasting statements (*"Bukan sekadar X, tapi Y"*).
- **Emoji Stuffing**: Avoid emoji bullets and hype decoration.
